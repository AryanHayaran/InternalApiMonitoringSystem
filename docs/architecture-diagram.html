Reading the topology

Both groups read every message

Group 1 and Group 2 consume the same topic independently, with separate offsets. If the rollup falls behind, alerting is completely unaffected. That isolation is the entire reason a second group exists rather than a second function call.

Within a group, partitions divide

Group 1's two consumers take one partition each. A third consumer would sit idle — a partition is only ever assigned to one consumer per group. Group 2's single consumer takes both.

What is genuinely new

  • Partitions 1 → 2
